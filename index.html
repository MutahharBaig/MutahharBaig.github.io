01 // about

From signal in to intelligence out

I work at the layer where machine learning meets bare metal. My day-to-day spans training and compressing deep-learning models — quantization, pruning, TensorFlow Lite, ONNX, RKNN — and writing the C/C++ firmware that runs them in real time on microcontrollers and edge processors.

That range comes from doing both ends seriously: FreeRTOS multi-tasking firmware on the ESP32-S3, speech-to-text pipelines on Hugging Face Spaces, computer-vision systems for precision agriculture, and biomedical signal processing on ECG and PCG data.

I won the 2025 Open House competition of the Electronics Engineering Department at UET Taxila with an autonomous AI-powered Agri-Rover, and my final-year project was selected for the All Punjab Expo 2025.
